## Ch3 Gaming Taxes (pp.35–41)

- Settled markers taxed only on amount collected (NRS 363.371); reasons to settle: induce partial payment, compromise dispute, retain patron p.36

## Ch5 Currency Reporting (pp.61–82)  [rules as of 2003 — app must make thresholds configurable]
- 1985 casinos = financial institutions under Title 31 BSA; report > $10,000 cash in 24h; multiple transactions aggregated if casino has knowledge pp.61–62
- Jurisdictions: NV chose Reg 6A self-regulation (still files with IRS); AC/PR Title 31 p.62
- Reg 6A prohibited: cash-for-cash exchange > $3,000 (incl foreign currency; not chips); casino check for cash > $3,000 (except verified winnings); checks/wires for winnings to third party p.63
- CTRC-N Form 8852 (Fig 5.1) vs Form 103 (Fig 5.2) p.63
- MTL: record every cash transaction > $3,000 (and sub-$3,000 same-patron amounts combining > $3,000 e.g. two $2,000 jackpots → log $4,000) p.63; MTL fields: patron description/name, table #, time/date/type/amount, signature; 24h with casino-chosen cutoff; supervisors review through shift; to accounting p.69–71
- CTRC-N 10 fields: date/time, amount, type, name, permanent address, ID method & doc number, SSN, DOB + account #, preparer signature, reviewer signature (different employee) pp.63–68
- Cash-in examples (5): losing cash wager, keno/R&S cash wager, chip purchase, marker payment, front money deposit; cash-out (8): chip redemption, winning ticket, front money withdrawal, check cashing/credit card advance, slot club points cash-out, wire transfer cash, tournament winnings, marketing incentive/travel reimbursement p.68
- Never combine cash-in with cash-out p.69; customer refusing ID → can't cash out >$10k chips; after-the-fact report; refuse → BAR from all gaming at all affiliated NV casinos until info provided p.69
- Like-kind aggregation only (Reg 6A), except SINGLE VISIT RULE: all same-direction cash at one location in one continuous appearance (example $4k+$4k chips + $4k losing wager at one table → $12k CTRC-N) p.71
- Front money/safekeeping: segregate same cash, or record denominations and return same p.71
- Barred patron log p.72; casino-chosen 24h window benefits p.72; like-kind separation lets player do $10k each of chips, money play, markers, R&S, front money without report p.72
- ID on file reuse; no need to inform patron p.72–73; AC/PR prohibit cash betting and pit marker redemptions p.73
- 6A MICS monitoring: internal audit quarterly interviews, annual testing w/ samples from each quarter; branch office walk-throughs if > $1M cash transactions; exceptions to mgmt; independent accountant annual report (Reg 6A.110), 4 hrs unannounced observation/quarter p.73–74
- Title 31: cash-for-cash allowed; aggregate cash-ins and cash-outs separately across the gaming day (e.g., $5,000 front money + $5,001 pit chips → report); AC casinos must use computer systems to aggregate; daily exception report p.74
- 1997 changes: thresholds $2,500→$3,000; SAR added; training; funds transfers; single visit rule; "10 and 2 rule" (Reg 6A applies only if > $10M gross gaming revenue AND > $2M table win) p.75
- SARC (Title 31, eff 25 Mar 2003): ≥ $5,000 and (illegal funds / avoid CTR / no lawful purpose / facilitate crime); casinos ≥ $1M gross gaming revenue; must not tell patron; file within 30 days of detection (+30 to identify suspect, max 60) p.78
- Fig 5.6 comparison Title 31 vs Reg 6A p.82
- Title 26 non-gaming (hotel, retail, F&B): Form 8300; 12-month aggregation; cash incl cashier's checks/bank drafts/money orders/traveler's checks ≤ $10,000 face (e.g. $6k cash + $7k cashier's check jewelry → file); not personal checks; annual statement to person by Jan 31; Pub 1544; penalty greater of $25,000 or cash amount up to $100,000 per unfiled form pp.81–82

- ROULETTE: chips (non-negotiable, color per player, 15 stacks × 20); double zero 38 pockets; Table 8.1 bets A–L & payouts (straight 35:1; column/dozen 2:1; color/odd-even/high-low 1:1; split 17:1; street 11:1; corner 8:1; 1st five 6:1; alley/six-line 5:1; basket 11:1); HA 5.26% except 1st five 7.89%; single zero 2.7% p.150–152

- BACCARAT: values, naturals 8/9, player draws on 0–5; banker draws on 0–2; banker third-card table (3: draw unless player 3rd = 8; 4: draw 2–7; 5: draw 4–7; 6: draw 6–7; 7 stands); banker draws <6 if player stands; banker paid 0.95:1 → 1.06% p.156–158
